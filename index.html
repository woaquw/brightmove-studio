      FAQ

      Questions before your first class

      I have never done Pilates. Can I join?

      Yes. Start with Mat Pilates or the free trial class: the instructor explains every exercise and offers easier options.

      What should I bring?

      Comfortable clothes and grip socks (we sell them at the front desk). Mats, towels and water are provided.

      How do I cancel a booking?

      Cancel up to 12 hours before the class and the credit returns to your account. Later cancellations count as used.

      Is Pilates safe during pregnancy or after an injury?

      Often yes, with adjustments. Tell us in the booking form and check with your doctor first; we may suggest a private session to start.
